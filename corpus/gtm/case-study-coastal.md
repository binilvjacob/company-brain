# Case study — Coastal Dental Collective

**Profile:** 12 locations, Eaglesoft, small central RCM team (4). Growth tier.
Live since February 2026. The "skeptical buyer" reference.

## Before
CFO-driven purchase with a hostile-to-vendors RCM lead. Denials were the
board-level pain: eligibility-related denials were costing them roughly
**$84,000/quarter** in written-off or reworked claims (their finance team's
number, from the lookback audit we ran in January).

## After (2 quarters)
- Recovered/prevented eligibility-denial value: their finance team attributes
  **$84k/quarter → $31k/quarter** written off, with the delta credited to
  pre-treatment clause and frequency flags (MTC catches alone: 40+ cases).
- RCM lead converted from skeptic to reference — the turning point was
  INC-2026-032 (our write-back double-posting incident hit 2 of their
  locations): same-day cleanup, full incident summary, and the audit diff.
  Her line: "vendors show you who they are when they break something."
- Verification coverage of schedule: 99% at 24h-before.

## Lessons for sales
1. The lookback audit converted this deal — their own denials, quantified,
   beat every demo.
2. An incident handled transparently *strengthened* the account. Never hide
   the postmortem from a customer it touched.

## Reference status
Referenceable specifically for skeptical RCM leaders and for write-back
safety questions. Route via Chris; pair with the INC-2026-032 postmortem when
the audience is technical.
