# Case study — Bright Smile Dental Group

**Profile:** 8 locations, Dentrix, no central RCM team — front desks verified
between patients. Starter → Growth tier. Live since December 2025.

## Before
Each front desk spent **~11 hours/week** on verification calls and portals.
Verification depth was inconsistent: frequency history usually skipped under
time pressure, which surfaced as awkward checkout conversations and write-offs
("your insurance said no after all").

## After (90-day check)
- Front-desk verification time: ~11 h/wk/office → **under 2 h/wk** (handling
  our flagged items and patient questions only).
- Frequency-limitation surprises at checkout: the metric they actually
  wanted — anecdotally near-zero; formally, write-offs tagged
  "eligibility" down **61%** quarter over quarter.
- Offices stopped keeping private payer-quirk cheat sheets; the verification
  note in Dentrix became the source of truth.

## Quote (practice administrator)
"The write-back is the magic. Nobody logs into anything new — the answer is
just in the chart where the front desk already looks."

## Watch-outs from onboarding
Dentrix note-length caps required the truncate-with-link pattern; Sunday
maintenance windows mean Monday-morning schedules verify by 6am, not
overnight. Both handled, both worth setting expectations on in sales.

## Reference status
Referenceable for group practices; administrator happy to talk to
owner-dentists.
