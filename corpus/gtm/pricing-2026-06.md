# Pricing & packaging — CURRENT (effective 2026-06-14)

Per-verification pricing, monthly invoicing, no setup fees. This sheet
supersedes the November 2025 one-pager — retire any deck still quoting it.

## Tiers
- **Starter** — $1.10/verification, up to 1,500/mo. Group practices; email
  support; standard SLA.
- **Growth** — **$0.85/verification, 3,000/mo minimum commit.** The DSO
  workhorse tier. Priority lane included, dashboard API, named CSM.
- **Scale** — custom, >15,000/mo. Volume steps down from $0.75; includes
  claims-beta bundle option (+$0.35/claim tracked while in beta), SFTP
  exports, quarterly business reviews.

## Rules of engagement
- Discounting: up to 10% on annual prepay (AE discretion), anything beyond
  that is a Jofin approval with a note in the CRM.
- The 3,000 minimum on Growth is a floor, not a target — prospects doing
  ~2,000/mo belong on Starter until they grow into it; do not stretch them
  into commits they'll resent.
- Pilots (30 days, 1 location) bill at the tier rate with the minimum waived.

## Price-change note (June 2026)
Growth moved from $0.95 → $0.85 with the 3k minimum added. Rationale: DSO
deals were stalling at ~$0.90 blended; the minimum protects revenue while the
headline rate wins the comparison against offshore-VA cost-per-verification.
Existing Growth customers were grandfathered at $0.95 **without** a minimum
until their renewal.
