# ICP definition

## Primary: emerging DSOs (10–50 locations)
Central RCM team drowning in verification volume; growth outpacing hiring.
Buyer: **VP RCM / Director of RCM**, economic buyer often CFO. Trigger events:
acquisition of new locations, RCM lead turnover, denial-rate board question.
Why they buy: capacity without headcount, standardization across acquired
practices' messy processes, and audit-trail defensibility.

## Secondary: group practices (5–10 clinics)
No dedicated RCM function; front desks verify between patients or a small
central team of 2–3. Buyer: owner-dentist or practice administrator. Why they
buy: front-desk time back (hours/week/office) and fewer eligibility surprises
at checkout. Shorter cycle, smaller ACV, high referral energy.

## Also-fits
RCM/billing service companies serving dental (white-label conversations —
handle case-by-case, margin math differs).

## Explicit non-ICP (today)
Single-location practices (CAC math fails), Medicaid-dominant practices
(Denti-Cal/state programs unsupported — see product limitations), oral-surgery
-only groups with medical-primary billing (cross-billing out of scope).

## Qualification shorthand (BANT-ish, dental flavor)
Volume ≥1,500 verifications/mo · a named person who owns denials · PMS in our
four (Dentrix, Open Dental, Eaglesoft, Curve) · willingness to run the
lookback audit. Two of four = keep working it; all four = pilot-qualified.
