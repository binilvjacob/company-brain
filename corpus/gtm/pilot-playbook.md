# Pilot playbook

Standard motion after a lookback-audit readout. 30 days, one location, tier
rate with minimum waived.

## Week 0 — setup
PMS connection (read + dry-run write-back), payer credential enrollment for
the location's top payers, success criteria **written into the CRM before
kickoff**: typically schedule-coverage %, backlog time, and 2–3
practice-picked "dread payers" to prove.

## Weeks 1–2 — dry-run mode
We verify the full schedule; write-back stages for practice approval
(`writeback-dry-run` on). Daily 10-min check-in with the practice admin the
first week — this call is where trust is built, do not skip it. Specialists
watch this account's queue with fresh-payer QA (100% dual review).

## Weeks 3–4 — live write-back
Dry-run off once the practice has approved two weeks of postings without
corrections beyond threshold. Priority lane demonstrated live on a same-day
add-on. Mid-pilot readout at day 21 with the dashboard.

## Exit readout (day 30)
Against the written criteria, plus: exceptions sample walked through *with*
the flags shown — prospects convert on seeing the system say "I wasn't sure,
here's why" (mirrors what our founders call the no-silent-mistakes
principle). Decision meeting scheduled before the readout, never after.

## Pilot kill criteria (be honest, kill fast)
Schedule sync unreliable on their infra, payer mix >30% unsupported programs,
or practice won't staff the approval loop in dry-run weeks. A killed pilot
with a clear reason preserves the relationship; a zombie pilot doesn't.
