# Pricing one-pager (November 2025)

Per-verification pricing, monthly invoicing, no setup fees.

## Tiers
- **Starter** — $1.10/verification, up to 1,500/mo.
- **Growth** — $0.95/verification, no minimum.
- **Scale** — custom above 15,000/mo.

## Positioning notes
Anchor against the fully-loaded cost of manual verification (staff time at
15–40 min per patient) rather than against software line items. The
per-verification unit makes the build-vs-buy spreadsheet easy for CFOs.

Pilots: 30 days, one location, tier rate.
