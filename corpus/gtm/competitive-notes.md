# Competitive landscape notes

Three archetypes show up in deals. (Named companies here are the archetype
leaders we actually lose/win against; keep claims factual — quote the audit,
not adjectives.)

## VerifyIQ — the offshore-hybrid archetype
VA teams with a workflow layer; wins on sticker price ($0.60–0.75/verif
advertised). Turnaround 24–48h, no provenance, quality varies by team.
**How we win:** same-morning turnaround, cost per *correct* verification, and
the audit trail. Where we lose: price-only buyers under ~1,000/mo volume — let
those go honestly.

## DentaCheck — the portal-scraper archetype
Software-only portal scraping, no voice capability, no human layer. Falls over
exactly where verification is hard: Guardian (history is voice-only), Cigna
DHMO (PDF capitation schedules), COB ordering. Their demo looks identical to
ours on easy payers.
**How we win:** make the prospect pick their 20 hardest verifications for the
lookback audit — Guardian and DHMO members expose the gap in one meeting.
Where we lose: prospects whose payer mix is genuinely easy (rare at DSO
scale).

## Clearinghouse EDI eligibility — the "good enough" incumbent
Not a company, a habit: the 270/271 checkbox in their existing stack.
**How we win:** the 271 can't answer frequency history, pooled buckets,
clauses, downgrades — the actual denial causes. We retired our own EDI-first
path for exactly this reason (feature-flag registry); saying "we tried it"
lands well with technical buyers.

## Rules
No FUD, no invented numbers about competitors in front of prospects. If asked
something we don't know about a competitor, say so and log it — do not
improvise.
