# Case study — Summit Dental Partners

**Profile:** 23 locations, Open Dental, central RCM team of 9. Growth-tier.
Live since October 2025.

## Before
Verification backlog averaged **3.2 days**; same-day and next-day appointments
routinely went unverified. Two RCM staff did nothing but portal checks.
Eligibility-related denials: 9.8% of claims. New-location onboarding meant
re-teaching payer quirks each acquisition.

## After (measured at 6 months)
- Backlog: 3.2 days → **same-morning** for the full schedule.
- Eligibility-related denial rate on verified appointments: **down 38%**
  (9.8% → 6.1%, tracking toward the <5% target as older claims wash through).
- **2 FTEs redeployed** from portal-checking to denial follow-up and patient
  cost conversations — their words: "we finally work the queue instead of the
  portals."
- Priority lane SLA hit rate at their busiest location: 96%.

## What they'd tell a peer (from the QBR)
"The flags are the feature. When it isn't sure, it says so and shows why —
my team trusts the green ones because the yellow ones exist."
— VP Revenue Cycle

## Expansion
3 locations in the claims beta since June 2026; evaluating Scale tier at
renewal given acquisition pipeline.

## Reference status
**Referenceable** for DSO prospects (route via Chris; VP RCM prefers 30-min
calls, no site visits during quarter-close months).
