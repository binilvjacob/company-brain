# Discovery call script

30 minutes. Goal is one number and one commitment: their verification volume,
and a yes to the lookback audit. Demo only if they ask.

## Opening (5 min)
Their world first: "Walk me through what happens between a patient booking and
sitting in the chair, insurance-wise." Listen for who does verification, how
long before the appointment, and what happens when it's skipped.

## The four discovery questions (15 min)
1. **Volume & coverage:** "How many appointments a month, and what share get a
   real benefits check versus a coverage-only glance?" (Sizes the deal;
   exposes the depth gap.)
2. **Denials:** "What % of your denials are eligibility-related, and who eats
   the rework?" If they don't know the number, that IS the finding — the
   lookback audit will produce it.
3. **The hard payers:** "Which payers does your team dread?" Guardian, DHMO
   plans, and FEP answers tell you the pain is real and map to our strengths.
4. **Prior attempts:** "What have you tried — clearinghouse eligibility, VAs,
   more hires?" Sets up the honest comparison, not a bake-off.

## The ask (5 min)
The lookback audit: "Give us your last 500 verifications; we'll re-run them
and show you what a deeper check would have caught, with dollar estimates on
the denials. Two weeks, no charge, no integration — export only." Close on a
date for the readout.

## Red flags to log honestly
Medicaid-dominant mix (unsupported), volume under ~800/mo (ROI thin — say so),
PMS outside our four, or a buyer who wants "no humans anywhere" (our model is
explicitly human-in-the-loop; misaligned expectations churn).
