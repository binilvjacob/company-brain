# Expense policy

Default: spend company money like it's yours and you're slightly frugal.

## Receipts & approvals
- **Under $50 (or ₹4,000): no receipt needed**, description required.
- $50–$500: receipt in the expense tool within 30 days.
- Over $500 or anything recurring: manager pre-approval.
- Software/tools: Maya pre-approval regardless of amount (vendor list &
  security review — see vendor list doc).

## Travel
Booked via **TravelPerk**. Economy flights (premium economy above 6h with
manager approval), hotels at the tool's in-policy tier, ground transport
actuals. Customer-facing travel (pilots, QBRs, conferences) sits on the GTM
budget; book it under the account's trip label.

## Meals
Solo travel meals: actuals up to $60/day. Team meals: lead approves.
Customer meals: fine within reason; log attendees.

## Equipment
Laptop refresh cycle **3 years** (see IT section of onboarding doc);
peripherals under $150 self-serve once per year.

## Not reimbursable
Alcohol-only receipts, airline seat upgrades outside policy, home-office
furniture above the onboarding stipend, anything for someone outside the
company without a business purpose logged.
