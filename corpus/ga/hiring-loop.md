# Hiring loop & interview standards

Same skeleton for every role; the practical stage flexes by function.

## Stages
1. **Recruiter screen (30 min)** — motivation, logistics, comp range shared
   openly (bands are internal-only, ranges are candidate-visible).
2. **Hiring manager (45 min)** — depth on the last two years of real work.
3. **Practical** — role-shaped: eng pairs on a real-ish problem; ops candidates
   work 3 sample exceptions with a playbook; GTM runs a discovery role-play;
   founder's-office style roles get a **time-boxed take-home with a writeup**,
   because the writeup shows judgment better than any interview hour.
4. **Panel (2×45 min)** — cross-functional; one interviewer is always from the
   team the role serves, not just the team it sits in.
5. **Founder chat (30 min)** — values, expectations both directions.

## Standards
- Structured scorecards within 24h; "hire" requires evidence, not vibes.
- Practical work is either paid (>4h expected effort) or scoped under 4h.
- Candidates hear a decision within 5 business days of the panel — recruiting
  debt compounds worse than code debt.
- References for final candidates: 2, done by the hiring manager personally.

## What we screen for everywhere
Comfort saying "I don't know"; specific ownership stories ("I" not "we" at
the decision points); writing quality (every role here writes).
