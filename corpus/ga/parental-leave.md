# Parental leave policy

Applies to full-time employees after 90 days of employment. Contractors are
out of scope (per-contract terms apply).

## Leave
- **Primary caregiver: 16 weeks paid**, usable within 12 months of
  birth/adoption, splittable into up to two blocks.
- **Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks paid**, same 12-month window.
- Where statutory entitlements (India maternity benefit, US state programs)
  exceed this policy, the statutory scheme applies and we top up pay to 100%
  for the policy duration.

## Return & ramp
Two weeks at 60% load on return (full pay), no on-call/priority-pager duty for
the first 30 days back in ops and eng rotations.

## While on leave
No work expectation, access optional. Role or an equivalent is guaranteed on
return; performance cycles treat leave as neutral time.

## How to plan it
Tell Maya and your manager ~3 months ahead where possible; ops and eng leads
own the coverage plan (rota backfill, account handoffs) so the leave starts
clean.
