# Compensation bands FY2026 — LEADERSHIP ONLY

Internal bands. Candidate-visible *ranges* are derived from these by
recruiting; the bands themselves do not leave leadership.

## India (Kochi) — annual fixed, INR
- RCM Specialist L1: ₹6–8 LPA · L2 (senior): ₹9–12 LPA
- Ops Lead: ₹18–24 LPA
- Engineer L2: ₹18–26 LPA · L3: ₹28–42 LPA · L4 (staff): ₹45–60 LPA
- Product Manager: ₹30–45 LPA

## US — annual base, USD
- Account Executive: $95–120k base, OTE 2× at plan
- CSM: $85–105k
- GTM Lead: $140–170k base

## Equity
Options by level: specialists 0.01–0.03%, engineers L2–L4 0.05–0.4%, leads
0.3–0.8%, executive hires case-by-case. Standard 4-year vest, 1-year cliff,
10-year exercise window.

## Rules
- Offers at or below band midpoint without approval; above midpoint needs
  Jofin + Maya sign-off with a written rationale.
- No off-band offers to close a candidate "just this once" — that is how
  bands die.
- Annual review each March against market data; off-cycle adjustments only
  for role changes.
