# PTO & holidays policy

Applies to full-time employees (India and US). Contractor arrangements are per
contract — this policy does not apply to contractors.

## PTO
**20 working days per calendar year**, accrued monthly (1.67/mo), usable from
day one against accrual. Carryover: up to 5 days into Q1 of the next year,
expiring March 31. No payout of unused PTO except where law requires.

## Holidays
**10 company holidays per year**, split-calendar: a shared core (New Year,
plus 4 common days) and a regional set (India national/regional days for the
Kochi office; US federal days for US-based GTM). The exception-queue rota runs
on customer (US practice) business days — ops staffing on India holidays that
are US working days is planned by Priya with comp-days in lieu.

## Sick time
Separate from PTO: 8 days/year, no accrual math, doctor's note only beyond 3
consecutive days.

## How to take it
Request in the HR tool ≥2 weeks ahead for 3+ days (ops: also the roster
sheet). Same-week requests need manager sign-off. Nobody's PTO is "approved
subject to workload" after it's on the calendar two weeks — plan around it.
