# New-hire onboarding — day 1 to week 2

## Day 1
Accounts (email, Slack, HR tool), laptop (3-year refresh cycle; home-office
stipend for remote), **HIPAA onboarding module before any system access
beyond email**, team lunch (office) or scheduled video welcome (remote).
Manager posts an intro in #general with what you'll own.

## Week 1 — everyone shadows the queue
Regardless of role, every new hire spends **half a day watching the exception
queue** with a specialist. GTM sells it, eng builds around it, product
roadmaps it — you should have seen it. Watch: what the flags look like, what
the specialist checks, what a good resolution note reads like.

## Week 1 — role tracks
- Ops: playbook bootcamp (top-7 payers), 20 supervised exceptions, QA rubric
  walkthrough. Queue access unlocks after HIPAA extended module.
- Eng: local stack up day 1, first PR by day 3 (docs or tests count),
  incident-postmortem reading list (start with INC-2026-032).
- GTM: lookback-audit readout recording, objection guide, listen to 5
  discovery calls, shadow 2 live.
- Product/G&A: customer dashboard tour + 3 practice-persona interviews from
  the research library.

## Week 2
Own something small end-to-end and write it up: a playbook update, a nav-spec
fix, a discovery call section, a policy clarification. The writeup habit is
the onboarding goal — this company runs on written decisions.

## 30-day check
Manager + Maya: what surprised you, what's undocumented that you needed, what
should the next hire get that you didn't. Answers feed this doc.
