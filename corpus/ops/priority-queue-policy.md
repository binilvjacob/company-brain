# Priority lane policy (same-day appointments)

## What qualifies
- Appointment today (auto-flagged from the PMS schedule sync), or
- Practice manually requests rush via the portal, or
- Walk-in emergency where the practice calls us.

## SLA
30 minutes flag-to-resolution, measured in business hours of the practice's
timezone. Standard lane remains 4 business hours.

## How to work it
Skip the normal ladder. Go straight to the fastest authoritative source —
usually voice for accumulators/history, portal for provisions. It is
acceptable to resolve a priority item with a narrower answer: verify the
procedures on today's schedule, mark the rest `deferred-standard`, and let the
standard lane finish the full verification.

## Staffing
One specialist per shift carries the priority pager. Rotation is weekly;
swaps are self-serve in the roster sheet as long as coverage is continuous.

## July 2026 performance
Priority volume ~6% of exceptions; SLA hit rate 93.4% (target 95). The misses
cluster on Guardian history calls (voice-only payer + hold time) — batching
proposal under review with eng for a dedicated priority voice worker.
